About Eastokyo

Tokyo,
Chosen Carefully

Eastokyo is an independent magazine about the places, people and experiences that make Tokyo worth exploring again and again.

Useful enough to guide a day. Beautiful enough to keep.

We cover exceptional restaurants, distinctive hotels, art and design, nightlife, independent shops and neighborhoods worth exploring slowly. Eastokyo is created in eastern Tokyo but covers the entire city.

We believe a strong recommendation should be specific. We explain what makes a place memorable, who it is for and why it deserves a reader’s time. Our stories are concise by design and supported by original photography.

How We Work

Independent judgment, clear disclosures and genuine enthusiasm for Tokyo.

01

Choose Carefully

We feature places because they are distinctive, memorable and genuinely worth a reader’s time—not simply because they are fashionable or famous.

02

Be Useful

Every guide and review should help someone make a decision, plan a day or discover something they would otherwise miss.

03

Be Transparent

Editorial coverage, hosted experiences and paid partnerships are labeled clearly. Commercial support never disguises itself as independent reporting.

Issue No. 01

The first issue is deliberately compact: seven focused stories spanning food, hotels, neighborhoods, nightlife, culture and the people who make Tokyo interesting.

Eastokyo is founded, written and photographed by James Simmons in Tokyo, with future contributions from chefs, artists, designers, hoteliers and other people who know the city intimately.
