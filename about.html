About the publication

Journalism for the English teacher's world in Japan.

Eastokyo examines the people, schools, working conditions and decisions that shape English education. It is published by Englishire but maintains an independent editorial purpose.

The profession deserves serious attention.

English teaching is often discussed as an entry route to Japan, a staffing problem or a classroom technique. Eastokyo treats it as a profession with consequences: for teachers building lives here, for schools making long-term decisions and for learners whose education depends on continuity and judgment.

The magazine covers hiring, retention, management, working conditions, professional identity, classroom continuity and the systems surrounding the work.

One institution. Two different jobs.

Englishire supports schools with temporary English teacher cover. Eastokyo reports on the profession around that work. The relationship is visible by design; the purposes remain distinct.
