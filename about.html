About Eastokyo Education ReviewPublished in Tokyo · For the profession

THE WORK BEHIND THE LESSON.

Eastokyo exists to examine English teaching in Japan as a profession, an institution and a lived working world—not merely as a route into the country or a timetable problem to solve.

Why this publication exists

The classroom is the visible part. The profession is everything holding it together.

Behind each lesson are hiring decisions, working conditions, management systems, visa realities, career choices, institutional memory and the difficult question of what schools owe both learners and teachers.

Those forces are often discussed separately—or not at all. Eastokyo brings them into one editorial field and treats them with the seriousness normally reserved for established professions.

The publisher relationship

ONE IDENTITY. TWO DIFFERENT RESPONSIBILITIES.

Englishire provides temporary English teacher cover for Tokyo schools. Eastokyo reports on the profession surrounding that work.

The connection is visible because hiding it would be less trustworthy. The distinction matters because service interests must not determine editorial conclusions.

EnglishireSupports schools in practice through temporary teacher cover.
EastokyoExamines the profession through reporting, essays and case studies.

How the publication works

Standards before volume.

Read the full editorial policy →
01

Purpose before output

A story should clarify something important about the profession. Publishing frequency is not a sufficient reason to publish.

02

Evidence before certainty

Reporting distinguishes documented fact, informed interpretation, personal experience and unresolved questions.

03

Fairness without softness

Institutions and individuals deserve context and an opportunity to respond. They do not receive protection from justified scrutiny.

04

Clear relationships

Commercial support, professional connections and material conflicts are disclosed where they could affect reader judgment.

05

Corrections in public

Meaningful errors are corrected promptly and transparently rather than quietly edited into disappearance.

06

Japan, not abstraction

The magazine is rooted in the actual institutions, laws, commutes, classrooms and working lives of Japan.
