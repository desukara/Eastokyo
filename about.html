About Eastokyo

Tokyo at
Its Best

Eastokyo is an independent travel and culture magazine devoted to the places, people and experiences that make Tokyo the greatest city on Earth.

A carefully chosen guide to one of the world's most exciting cities.

We cover exceptional restaurants, distinctive hotels, inspiring art and design, unforgettable nightlife, independent shops and neighborhoods worth exploring slowly.

Tokyo rewards curiosity. A tiny counter can serve the meal of a lifetime. A quiet side street can lead to an extraordinary gallery, bar or shop. A single neighborhood can feel like a complete city of its own. Eastokyo exists to find those experiences and share them with readers who want more than a checklist of famous attractions.
