THE PROFESSION

Reporting and essays on English teachers, schools, hiring, retention, classroom continuity and professional life in Japan.

English teaching, examined properly.

Eastokyo Magazine is Englishire's digital editorial publication for the English-teaching profession. It brings together serious reporting on teachers, schools, staffing, management and the systems shaping classroom life.

The publication and the service remain distinct in purpose, but share one identity: Englishire supports schools in practice; Eastokyo reports on the profession.
