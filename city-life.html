Eastokyo / City Life

HOW TOKYO WORKS

Education, employment, institutions and the people whose decisions shape everyday life in the city.

A city is more than the places visitors see.

City Life brings the former Englishire Journal into Eastokyo Magazine as a permanent editorial department. These essays examine schools, staffing, migration, workplace expectations and institutional continuity in Japan.

The collection retains its seriousness and specialist knowledge, but now sits inside a broader magazine about Tokyo and the people who make it function.

Essay 03 · Retention

The Real Cost of English Teacher Turnover

Essay 04 · Continuity
