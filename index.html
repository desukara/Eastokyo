The publication

The English teachers' magazine of Tokyo and Japan.

Eastokyo Education Review is Englishire's independent editorial publication for the English-teaching profession. It reports on the people who teach, the schools that employ them, the systems that shape their work and the decisions that determine whether classrooms thrive or struggle.

We cover hiring, retention, school operations, working conditions, professional identity, classroom continuity and the realities of building a teaching career in Tokyo and across Japan.
