From the Editor

A small first issue with a clear point of view.

Eastokyo is an independent travel and culture magazine created in Tokyo and covering the whole city. We choose places for their quality, character and sense of place—not because they are simply famous.

Issue No. 01 is deliberately concise: seven focused stories, original photography and useful recommendations designed to make readers want to experience Tokyo more deeply.
