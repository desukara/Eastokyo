What Eastokyo is

The English teacher's magazine for Japan.

Eastokyo reports on the profession behind English education in Japan: the people who teach, the schools that employ them, the systems that shape their work and the decisions that determine whether classrooms thrive or struggle.

We cover hiring, retention, school operations, working conditions, professional identity, classroom continuity and the realities of building a teaching career in Japan.

Eastokyo is published in partnership with Englishire, Tokyo's temporary English teacher-cover service.
