From the Editor

There is nowhere else quite like Tokyo.

Eastokyo is an independent travel and culture magazine devoted to the very best of the city. We cover memorable restaurants, beautiful hotels, independent shops, art, design, nightlife, neighborhoods and the people who give Tokyo its extraordinary character.

Our goal is simple: find places worth crossing the city for, tell their stories well, and make every issue feel like an invitation to experience Tokyo more deeply.
