Write with Eastokyo

YOUR VOICE BELONGS IN THE PROFESSION.

You do not need a journalism degree, a famous byline or twenty years in a staff room to begin.

You need a real observation, a useful question, an experience worth examining—or the curiosity to find out what is actually happening.

Aspiring teachers · working teachers · former teachers · school leaders · writers · researchers · photographers · thoughtful newcomers

We are not only looking for finished writers.

We are looking for people with something alive to bring into the room.

Maybe you have taught one difficult class and cannot stop thinking about what happened. Maybe you have spent fifteen years watching a school change. Maybe you are preparing to teach in Japan and have questions nobody answers honestly. Maybe you can report, photograph, interview, research or simply notice what others miss.

Eastokyo can help shape a strong idea into a publishable story. Standards matter. So does making space for new voices to develop.

How it begins

You do not need to arrive with the article already solved.

01

Tell us what is pulling at you.

A paragraph is enough: the question, experience, person or problem you keep returning to.

02

Explain why someone else should care.

Who would recognize themselves in it? What might a teacher, school or aspiring educator understand differently afterward?

03

Tell us what you can bring.

That may be direct experience, interviews, documents, photographs, research, access—or simply the willingness to report.

04

We shape the route together.

The idea may become an essay, reported feature, Q&A, column, visual story, anonymous source conversation or something neither of us has named yet.
