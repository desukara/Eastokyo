Trust and transparency

Editorial policy.

Readers should understand how a story was reported, what relationships may matter and where Eastokyo's role ends and Englishire's service role begins.

Independent conclusions. Visible relationships.

Eastokyo is published by Englishire, a temporary English teacher-cover service. That relationship is disclosed throughout the publication. Englishire may inform the magazine's understanding of schools and staffing, but it does not purchase favourable conclusions or suppress relevant criticism.

Editorial work is selected for public value to teachers, schools and the profession. Service enquiries, client matters and confidential information are not treated as reporting material without appropriate consent and justification.

01

Accuracy

Names, dates, quotations, employment claims and material facts are checked before publication.

02

Fairness

People and organisations facing meaningful criticism are given a reasonable opportunity to respond when appropriate.

03

Conflicts

Relevant professional, financial or personal relationships are disclosed and managed openly.

04

Corrections

Substantive errors are corrected promptly, with a note where the change affects the meaning of the story.

05

Sources and privacy

Source protection is considered against accuracy, fairness and the public value of the information.

06

Commercial work

Sponsored or commissioned material is labelled clearly and kept distinct from independent editorial judgment.

Corrections and concerns.

Send the article URL, the disputed passage, the proposed correction and supporting material. Editorial complaints are reviewed on their substance, not on the status of the person or organisation raising them.
